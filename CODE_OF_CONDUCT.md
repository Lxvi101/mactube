# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in MacTube a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's information.
- Help make the project a safe place for newcomers to contribute.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub's private advisory form](https://github.com/Lxvi101/mactube/security/advisories/new). Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
